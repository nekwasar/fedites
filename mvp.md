# Fedites — MVP Scope

The features approved for the first build: **112 features** across sections 1–13 of the [feature catalog](features.md). Everything not listed here is deferred to later phases (see [Deferred beyond MVP](#deferred-beyond-mvp)).

**Key scope decisions:**
- **Legacy family linking** is included but **private** by default.
- **WhatsApp bridge only** — no Telegram.
- **Newsletter builder** ships with **storable and uploadable templates**.

## 1. Identity & Verification (8)
- **Alumni verification** — Confirm members truly attended using year groups, records, photos, and trusted peer vouching.
- **Invitation codes** — Existing members invite classmates through secure codes, keeping the community authentic and trusted.
- **Profile builder** — Rich profiles with photos, bios, cities, careers, families, and favorite old school memories.
- **Set (year-group) tagging** — Every member belongs to a graduating set, enabling batch discussions, funds, and reunions.
- **Digital alumni ID** — A scannable membership card proving affiliation, usable for events, discounts, and venue check-ins.
- **Legacy family linking** *(private)* — Connect multi-generation families who attended the same school, celebrating alumni dynasties spanning decades.
- **Honorary membership** — Former teachers and staff receive special accounts, honoring their roles in everyone's lives.
- **Membership tiers** — Annual, life, and honorary tiers, each offering different dues, privileges, and voting rights.

## 2. Networking & Careers (10)
- **Member directory** — One searchable directory filterable by set, profession, industry, city, country, and personal interests.
- **Global alumni map** — Interactive world map showing where alumni live, work, study, and travel right now.
- **People you may know** — Smart suggestions resurfacing classmates and mutual connections you have not reconnected with yet.
- **Connections & follows** — Send connection requests or quietly follow interesting alumni to grow your professional network.
- **Alumni business directory** — Showcase alumni-owned businesses with services, contacts, opening hours, reviews, and special promotional offers.
- **Job board** — Alumni post openings and internships, always prioritizing fellow alumni and fresh graduates first.
- **Mentor office hours** — Members book one-on-one video time slots with senior professionals right inside the app.
- **Referrals & endorsements** — Request job referrals and skill endorsements from alumni already working at target companies.
- **Alumni near me** — Location radar discovering alumni living in, or currently visiting, your city right now.
- **City ambassadors** — Volunteer locals warmly welcome newly relocated alumni and coordinate regular city meetup gatherings.

## 3. Communication (10)
- **Direct messaging** — Private one-on-one chats supporting file attachments, voice notes, reactions, and message read receipts.
- **Group chats** — Persistent chats for sets, chapters, committees, and any spontaneously created special interest circle.
- **Discussion forums** — Threaded, searchable topic boards for debates, advice requests, announcements, and long-form member conversations.
- **Official announcements** — Broadcast executive notices that pin prominently and reach absolutely every single verified member.
- **Mentions & push alerts** — Tag members, who then receive instant push notifications across all their connected devices.
- **Email digests** — Automated weekly summaries of community activity, delivered neatly into every member's email inbox.
- **Newsletter builder** — Simple drag-and-drop builder for producing polished, branded association newsletters without hiring any designers.
  - Templates must be **storable and uploadable** (save, reuse, and import custom designs).
- **WhatsApp bridge** — Mirror key announcements automatically into the messaging groups members already use every day.
- **SMS fallback** — Reach members without smartphones through simple text-message alerts containing only the essential links.
- **Voice & video calls** — Native voice and video calling for mentorship sessions, committee meetings, and casual catch-ups.

## 4. Feed, Content & Memory (13)
- **News feed** — One single scrolling timeline mixing posts, photos, achievements, events, donations, and announcements together.
- **Reactions & threaded comments** — Expressive emoji reactions plus nested reply threads underneath every single published community post.
- **Alumni spotlights** — Regular interview features celebrating members' most inspiring careers, ventures, giving, and community contributions.
- **Long-form articles** — Members publish personal essays, memoirs, reflections, tributes, obituaries, and professional thought leadership pieces.
- **Throwback photo archive** — Digitized old school photos where members tag familiar faces and add captioned memories.
- **Yearbook digitization** — Every past yearbook scanned, made fully searchable, and tagged for nostalgic browsing sessions.
- **On this day** — Resurface nostalgic old photos and posts from previous years automatically every single morning.
- **Polls & surveys** — Quick member votes on executive decisions, preferences, trivia, and other playful fun questions.
- **School history timeline** — Interactive, scrollable timeline covering the school's founding, milestones, famous eras, and campus legends.
- **School wiki** — Collaboratively edited community encyclopedia of houses, teachers, traditions, rivalries, and famous school stories.
- **Slang dictionary** — Crowd-sourced glossary preserving the school's unique vocabulary, nicknames, chants, insults, and beloved phrases.
- **Media library** — Organized cloud storage for photos, videos, and documents, neatly sorted by school era.
- **Livestreaming** — Broadcast reunions, sports matches, and school assemblies live to alumni everywhere on earth.

## 5. Events & Reunions (6)
- **Event calendar** — One unified calendar listing every upcoming reunion, AGM, fundraiser, and local chapter meetup.
- **Reunion planning suite** — Committees manage RSVPs, budgets, task lists, schedules, and vendor coordination in one place.
- **QR door check-in** — Volunteers simply scan attendee QR codes at entrances, tracking live attendance counts automatically.
- **Virtual attendance** — Livestream access with live chat for members who simply cannot travel there physically.
- **Event photo wall** — Shared event galleries where attendees upload pictures and tag each other in realtime.
- **AI photo finder** — Smart facial search helping attendees instantly locate every single photo containing their face.

## 6. Groups & Chapters (6)
- **Geographic chapters** — City and country branches with their own admins, regular events, dues, and meetings.
- **Interest groups** — Casual member clubs for football, books, chess, photography, faith, fitness, and much more.
- **Professional guilds** — Professional industry circles for doctors, engineers, lawyers, founders, educators, creatives, and technologists alike.
- **Sports house revival** — Recreate the old school houses with friendly, modern inter-house competitions and live standings.
- **Committee workspaces** — Private workspaces with task boards, shared files, minutes, and deadlines for busy executives.
- **Group self-governance** — Every group elects its own admins, sets its own rules, and moderates itself.

## 7. Money & Giving (11)
- **Dues collection** — Automated annual dues collection with gentle reminders, instant receipts, and polite defaulter tracking.
- **Donations** — One-time or recurring giving toward approved projects, with instant thank-you receipt emails attached.
- **Fundraising campaigns** — Themed fundraising campaigns featuring live progress bars, deadlines, and publicly visible donor walls.
- **Peer-to-peer fundraising** — Members create personal fundraiser pages, then share them proudly across their personal networks.
- **In-memory & tribute giving** — Members donate in honor of deceased classmates, beloved teachers, or milestone birthday celebrations.
- **Pledge tracking** — Record promised contributions, then gently nudge their eventual fulfillment without any public embarrassment.
- **Transparent ledger** — Public, downloadable financial reports showing exactly how every single donated penny was spent.
- **Scholarship administration** — Endow, receive applications, screen, select, and disburse student scholarships entirely inside the platform.
- **Expense reimbursements** — Treasurers review and approve committee spending claims with digital receipt uploads securely attached.
- **Sponsorship portal** — Corporate partners sponsor events and newsletters, receiving visible recognition through tiered appreciation packages.
- **Multi-currency payments** — Members pay from anywhere worldwide using cards, bank transfers, and local mobile money.

## 8. Governance & Admin (12)
- **Admin dashboard** — One central console for managing members, content, finances, events, and all system settings.
- **Role-based permissions** — Granular roles such as president, treasurer, secretary, moderator, editor, and the ordinary member.
- **Elections** — Secure, anonymous voting for executive positions, with live results displayed as votes arrive.
- **Motions & resolutions** — Members formally propose, second, debate, and vote on important association motions entirely digitally.
- **AGM toolkit** — Quorum tracking, agendas, proxy forms, minutes publishing, and attendance tools, all neatly combined.
- **Constitution library** — Version-controlled governing documents and past amendments, accessible to every member at all times.
- **Suggestion box** — An anonymous ideas channel feeding member suggestions directly into the executive committee's planning.
- **Moderation toolkit** — Members report bad content; moderators mute, ban, and remove it, with audit trails.
- **Audit logs** — Every single administrative action is recorded transparently, thereby ensuring complete future organizational accountability.
- **Analytics dashboard** — Engagement, growth, and revenue dashboards that clearly guide smarter, data-driven association strategy decisions.
- **Data export** — Download complete member lists and financial records anytime, thus avoiding any platform lock-in.
- **Integrations** — Sync seamlessly with Mailchimp, Zapier, accounting software, and your existing public association website.

## 9. The School Bridge (10)
- **School news feed** — Official updates from the principal, plus warm glimpses of current daily school life.
- **School needs wishlist** — The school posts needed items, and alumni either fund or physically fulfill them.
- **Adopt-a-project** — Whole sets or classes can sponsor specific renovations, laboratories, or brand-new school libraries.
- **Student mentorship bridge** — Current students get matched with caring alumni mentors for academic and career guidance.
- **Career day volunteering** — Alumni sign up to speak at the school's annual career day events regularly.
- **Internship pipeline** — Current students apply directly for internships offered within the wider alumni business network.
- **Past questions bank** — An archive of past exams, notes, and study materials for the current students.
- **Teacher tributes** — Honor beloved retired and deceased teachers with heartfelt stories, old photos, and donations.
- **Facility booking** — Verified alumni rent school halls and sports fields for their own private events.
- **Records verification** — Employers can request officially verified enrollment and graduation records through the platform securely.

## 10. Milestones & Recognition (9)
- **Achievement awards** — An annual awards ceremony honoring members' professional, charitable, community, and lifetime service excellence.
- **Hall of fame** — A permanent, browsable showcase honoring the association's most distinguished members across all generations.
- **Badges & streaks** — Collectible earned badges for volunteering, donating, attending events, and other consistently active participation.
- **Birthday reminders** — Never forget any classmate's birthday again; send collective group wishes instantly and automatically.
- **Founding member status** — A permanent recognition status proudly awarded to early joiners and founding community pioneers.
- **Memorial pages** — Respectful tribute pages for deceased alumni, with digital condolence books and shared memories.
- **Condolence coordination** — Coordinated funeral attendance, group gifts, and welfare contributions during any painful bereavement period.
- **Anniversary countdowns** — Live countdown widgets and badges building excitement toward big upcoming reunion milestone celebrations.
- **Time capsules** — Sealed messages and photos, locked away until the milestone reunion anniversaries finally arrive.

## 12. Fun & Nostalgia (8) — the tender ones
- **School bell notifications** — An optional notification chime sampled from the actual old school bell sound recording.
- **School anthem player** — Stream treasured old recordings of anthems, hymns, and favorite school songs anytime anywhere.
- **Crest stickers & frames** — Custom school-crest emoji, stickers, and profile frames for all special celebratory occasions year-round.
- **Confetti moments** — Joyful celebratory confetti animations whenever donations land or milestone goals finally get completed.
- **"Remember when" threads** — Weekly nostalgia prompts that spark warm, collective memory storytelling sessions between school generations.
- **Recipe exchange** — Members recreate and share the famous old tuck-shop and dining-hall recipes together online.
- **Nostalgia radio** — Collaborative playlists of era-defining old songs from everyone's shared school days, curated together.
- **Letters to future self** — Write sealed notes to yourself, delivered automatically on your chosen future milestone dates.

## 13. Comfort, Trust & Craft (9)
- **Quiet hours & mutes** — Granular notification, mute, and quiet-hours controls carefully respecting every single member's work-life balance.
- **Dark mode** — A comfortable, eye-friendly dark theme for late-night nostalgic browsing sessions on any phone.
- **Accessibility** — Full screen-reader support, adjustable font sizes, and colorblind-friendly design choices throughout the app.
- **Low-bandwidth mode** — A lightweight, data-friendly interface mode for members with genuinely poor, unstable internet connectivity.
- **PWA & offline mode** — An installable progressive web app that functions offline on any modern phone anywhere.
- **Multi-language interface** — An interface fully localized to every single member's preferred language, wherever they live.
- **Privacy controls** — Granular visibility controls covering your own profile, contact details, and all activity information.
- **Two-factor authentication** — An optional second verification step protecting all member accounts against any hostile takeover.
- **Social share cards** — Beautiful share preview cards generated whenever posting alumni achievements to social media platforms.

---

## Deferred beyond MVP

- **Section 11 — Care, Welfare & Support:** excluded entirely (welfare dues, hospital visit coordination, grief circles, mental wellness circles, emergency assistance loans, legal aid network, disaster relief fundraising, medical fund).
- **Section 5:** ticketing & payments, icebreaker matchmaking, live Q&A and polling, scavenger hunts, hotel & travel deals, visa invitation letters, carpool coordination, watch parties.
- **Section 4:** stories, podcast & video channel.
- **Section 3:** auto-translation; Telegram bridge (WhatsApp bridge only).
- **Section 2:** digital business cards, anonymous salary insights, mentorship matching.
- **Section 7:** merchandise store, auctions & raffles, investment club, group savings circles, welfare fund.
- **Section 9:** legacy admission registry, retired teachers' welfare.
- **Section 10:** milestone celebrations, leaderboards (deferred — conflicts with the no-popularity-metrics rule).
- **Section 12:** daily trivia, games arcade, fantasy leagues (all games removed by decision).
- **Sections 14–15 (Growth & Platform, Marketplace):** not part of this MVP by default — the full catalog lives in [features.md](features.md) for a later phase.
